Independent retail, serving customers online since 2014.

JOSHUA D SULLIVAN is an independent, Wisconsin-based retail business selling general merchandise online — grocery, candy and snacks, and health & beauty products — to customers across the United States.

About

In business since 2014, JOSHUA D SULLIVAN is a sole proprietorship based in Sun Prairie, Wisconsin, operating as an e-commerce retailer on Amazon.com. We focus on everyday consumables and household categories, stocking quality products from established brands and fulfilling orders with reliable, responsive service.

As an independent seller, we work directly with wholesale distributors and brand partners to source the products our customers want.

Product Categories

  • Grocery & Snacks

    Pantry staples and everyday food items, including shelf-stable snacks and cooking essentials.

  • Candy & Confectionery

    National-brand candy, chocolates, and confections.

  • Health & Beauty

    Personal care, hair care, and everyday health products from trusted brands.

  • General Merchandise

    Household goods and other general merchandise from established manufacturers.

Wholesale Inquiries Welcome

We are actively looking to build relationships with wholesale distributors. If you represent a company that sells to independent retailers, please get in touch — we would be glad to discuss your product lines and account requirements.
